# OpenCTI Platform Usage Guide

## Dashboard
- **Purpose**: Provides an overview of threat data with visual widgets
- **Usage**: Monitor entity totals, relationships, and 24-hour changes
- **What you'll find**: Summary statistics, visual analytics, and platform status

## Activities & Knowledge
- **Purpose**: Categorizes security incidents and threat intelligence 
- **Usage**: Navigate between incident reports (Activities) and threat data (Knowledge)
- **What you'll find**: Organized access to reports, threat actors, campaigns, and victims

## Analysis
- **Purpose**: Contains reports and external references for threat intelligence
- **Usage**: View published reports, add investigation notes, and enrich knowledge
- **What you'll find**: Detailed reports with source attribution and analyst annotations

## Events
- **Purpose**: Records suspicious and malicious activities across networks
- **Usage**: Document findings and create associations between incidents
- **What you'll find**: Chronological security events with relationship mappings

## Observations
- **Purpose**: Catalogs technical elements and artifacts from cyber attacks
- **Usage**: Map threat events and correlate environment data against intel feeds
- **What you'll find**: IOCs, detection rules, and technical indicators

## Threats
- **Purpose**: Classifies all threatening entities to organizations
- **Usage**: Research threat actors, intrusion sets, and campaigns
- **What you'll find**: Profiles of malicious groups, APT patterns, and coordinated attack campaigns

## Arsenal
- **Purpose**: Lists attack-related items and legitimate tools used by adversaries
- **Usage**: Analyze malware, attack patterns, and vulnerabilities
- **What you'll find**: Malware details, TTPs, courses of action, and CVE information

## Entities
- **Purpose**: Categorizes information by sectors, countries, and organizations
- **Usage**: Enrich knowledge about targets and attack contexts
- **What you'll find**: Organizational profiles and victim classifications

## Entity Navigation Tabs
- **Overview**: General information, confidence levels, and key relationships
- **Knowledge**: Associated reports, indicators, and attack pattern timelines
- **Analysis**: Reports featuring the entity and investigation guidance
- **Indicators**: IOCs identified for threats and entities
- **Data**: Related files for export and communication
- **History**: Tracking of changes to elements, attributes, and relationships
