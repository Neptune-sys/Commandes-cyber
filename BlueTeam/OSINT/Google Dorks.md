# 🔍 Google Dorks for People Search

## 📧 Search by Email Address
- `"email@example.com"`  
- `intext:"email@example.com"`  
- `site:linkedin.com "email@example.com"`  
- `site:twitter.com "email@example.com"`  
- `site:pastebin.com "email@example.com"`  

## 🌍 Search by IP Address

- `site:whois.domaintools.com xxx.xxx.xxx.xxx`  
- `intitle:"index of" "xxx.xxx.xxx.xxx"`  

## 👤 Search by Username
- `"username"`

- `site:github.com "username"`  
- `site:twitter.com "username"`

## 🌐 Search by Domain
- `site:example.com`  
- `site:example.com filetype:pdf`  
- `site:example.com inurl:admin`  
- `site:example.com intitle:"index of"`  
- `site:example.com intext:"password"`  
- `site:example.com ext:sql | ext:txt | ext:log`  

## 🕵️‍♂️ Search for Sensitive Files
- `intitle:"index of" "passwd"`  
- `intitle:"index of" "config"`  
- `intitle:"index of" "backup"`  
- `filetype:log inurl:"password"`  
- `filetype:txt inurl:"email"`  
- `filetype:sql "INSERT INTO"`
